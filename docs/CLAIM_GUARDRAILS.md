# Claim boundaries

Describe LLM-AVChain as a proposed lifecycle-governed CTI architecture and runnable research proof of concept. Attribute the work to David Muñoz Sánchez and retain contributors' rights under the Apache 2.0 license.

- Distinguish the paper's design from implemented and tested behavior.
- Identify deterministic rules, test doubles, simulations and real local-model runs explicitly.
- Treat STIX/TAXII support as bounded interfaces, not ecosystem-wide certification or complete standards compliance.
- Do not claim production readiness, general malware protection, superior detection accuracy, complete privacy or security guarantees, or paper-scale empirical validation.
- Do not claim peer review, journal publication or patent status without separate evidence.
- Cite the exact software commit and dated experiment, including environment and limitations.

The [publication guide](PUBLICATION_AND_REPRODUCIBILITY.md) and [validation summary](../reports/README.md) provide the supported scope.
